How to play

Tap the colorful balls while the animated round plays.

01

Use one hand

Use one hand to tap a glowing ball in the six-target row. More than one ball can appear at a time.

02

Play for 30 seconds

Easy shows one ball at a time for 2 seconds. Hard shows a random group of two or three balls for 1.8 seconds. The next wave waits until the current one clears, then pauses briefly. A correct tap adds one point; an empty tap or expired ball removes one point.

03

Win with your difficulty target

Reach 10 points on Easy or 20 on Hard before the video ends. A lower score is a loss; the result is automatic.

04

Your score goes on the leaderboard

Your name, university, difficulty, score, and result are saved in this browser.

This browser-only game is modified for demonstration purposes. It simulates the original ESP32 hardware interaction, so anyone can play without a separate device.
